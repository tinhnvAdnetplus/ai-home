## The bug (measured by the reviewer, reproduce it first)
Default wizard answers (`getDefaultWizardAnswers()` in apps/api/resources/js/features/wizard/lib/default-answers.ts): 2 storeys, target area 30 tsubo (area band 30–35 tsubo = ~99–116 m2 total), LDK 18 jo, master bedroom 8 jo, 2 kids rooms 6 jo each, bath 1 tsubo, land 12 x 14 m (50.8 tsubo, coverage limit 60 %).
`mapAnswersToPlanInput(defaults)` -> `generatePlansSafe(input)` produces plan A with **floorAreaM2 = 158.99 (48.1 tsubo)**, 79.5 m2 per floor, **LDK = 46.4 m2 = 28.6 jo (asked 18)**, kids rooms 8.2 jo and 6.1 jo (asked 6 and 6), hall 13 m2 on 2F. Only an info-level warning `TOTAL_AREA_DEVIATION` is emitted. So the building footprint is driven by the LAND instead of the requested area, and the LDK/halls are stretched to fill it. Yet `packages/planner/scripts/conformance-sweep.ts` (T6.2) reports "100 % pass" for `floor_area` and `room_size_ldk`: **the checker is wrong/too lenient and was written by the same agent that wrote the planner. Do not trust it.**
